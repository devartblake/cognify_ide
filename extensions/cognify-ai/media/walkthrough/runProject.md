# Run and preview

**Cognify: Run Project**, or **▶ Run** in the status bar, starts your project's app. It runs on this machine or in the project's Dev Container, depending on how you created it.

Cognify waits until the app answers on its port, then opens it in a browser tab beside your code. The status bar then shows the port: click it to bring the preview back, or click **Stop** to end the app.

If the port is already taken, or the app doesn't start, Cognify tells you, and the terminal shows what happened.

Read the guide: [Run and preview](https://github.com/devartblake/cognify_ide/blob/main/DevDocs/Guides/RUN_AND_PREVIEW.md), including troubleshooting.
